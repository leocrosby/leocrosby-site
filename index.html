About Leo

"Where can I make the greatest Kingdom impact?"

In more than 30 years in ministry, I've held many different seats, but never the first one. I've been an event coordinator, a marketing director, and an executive pastor.

What I've learned is that God allows me to choose doors to walk through, sometimes not exactly where He was taking me, but He uses them and keeps me pointed in the right direction. For a long time, I thought the ultimate goal of that pathway was up some imaginary ladder to the senior pastor role.

"Not the #1 seat. A seat among equals."

What I found was that my impact on the Kingdom of God can be made from any seat, and sometimes the greatest impact is the one I'm already in. Not the #1 seat, but a seat among equals, as an executive pastor.

The Book

You don't need the final word to lead powerfully.

Leo is currently writing Influence from Any Seat, a practical, story-driven book for the leaders underneath the top seat who make the vision real. Your seat isn't a waiting room, and this book is the field guide for it.

01 Lead Yourself Influence starts with you.
  • Ch. 1: Make Mornings Matter. The discipline of filling your own tank before leadership empties it.
  • Ch. 2: Own Your Calendar. Deciding what deserves your best before urgency decides for you.
  • Ch. 3: When the Next Step Isn't Up. Separating growth from promotion, and status from contribution.
02 Lead Up Influence authority you don't possess.
  • Ch. 4: Ask More, Tell Less. Why questions build more influence than being right.
  • Ch. 5: Plant Seeds Before You Need the Harvest. Timing, patience, and playing the long game on ideas.
  • Ch. 6: Gracious Candor. Telling the truth to the leader above you without breaking trust.
03 Lead Across Influence people you don't control.
  • Ch. 7: Serve Sideways. Trading "what I need from you" for "how can I help you succeed."
  • Ch. 8: Ask Permission to Influence. Earning the right to speak into a peer's leadership.
  • Ch. 9: Don't Borrow Someone Else's Conflict. Refusing to become everyone's fixer.
04 Lead Down Influence by developing others.
  • Ch. 10: Don't Lead Everyone the Same. Different people require different approaches. Different doesn't mean deficient.
  • Ch. 11: Is It Skill or Is It Heart? Diagnosing the real problem before you try to correct it.
  • Ch. 12: Give Them Room to Get It Wrong. Coaching outcomes instead of directing every task.
05 Serve the Mission Your influence exists for something bigger than you.
  • Ch. 13: Get the Right Results. The difference between activity and alignment.
  • Ch. 14: Do Your Job. Advocating for change without letting frustration distract you from what's already yours to carry.
  • Ch. 15: Stay Long Enough to Matter. Why trust and credibility compound over years, not months.
06 Find Your Seat The highest seat isn't necessarily the seat of highest impact.
  • Ch. 16: Bloom Where You're Planted. Contentment without complacency.
  • Ch. 17: Find Your Greatest Contribution. Reframing success around stewardship and impact, not title.
