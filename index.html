About Leo

Someone had to make the vision real.

For most of his career, Leo Crosby has led without the top title, proof that influence was never about the seat. He's carried the weight of execution for leaders who needed someone they could trust with the truth and the task list.

That's the seat most organizations depend on and talk about least. Executive pastors. Chiefs of staff. Campus pastors. Operations leaders. The people who turn a Sunday vision into a Tuesday plan, who hold the tension between loyalty and honesty, and who carry organizational health on their shoulders without ever needing the credit.

“I've spent my career learning that influence isn't a title you're given. It's trust you build, one honest conversation at a time.”

Leo now works with executive pastors, ministry leaders, and organizational teams through speaking, coaching, and consulting. The goal is helping leaders build influence from any seat, with clarity, courage, and skill. Not theory. Work that's been tested in real rooms, with real stakes.

The Any-Seat Framework

A different definition of leadership from any seat.

Most people misunderstand what it means to lead without the title. Leo's framework starts by clearing that up.

Influence from Any Seat Is Not

  • Blind loyalty
  • Passive support
  • Saying yes to everything
  • Waiting for permission
  • Chasing a platform

Influence from Any Seat Is

  • Trusted leadership
  • Honest feedback
  • Courageous conversations
  • Stewardship of vision
  • Legacy over platform
01

Trust

The currency every leader spends, no matter the seat. Without it, influence is borrowed. With it, everything moves faster.

02

Clarity

Vision dies in vagueness. Leaders translate big ideas into plans a team can actually run on, whatever seat they lead from.

03

Influence

Leading up, down, and sideways, without a title to lean on. Earned through consistency, not position.

04

Execution

Vision without execution is just a nice conversation. This is where leaders earn their seat, whichever one it is.
