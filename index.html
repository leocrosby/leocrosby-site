The Any-Seat Framework

A different definition of leadership from any seat.

Most people misunderstand what it means to lead without the title. Leo's framework starts by clearing that up.

Influence from Any Seat Is Not

  • Blind loyalty
  • Passive support
  • Saying yes to everything
  • Waiting for permission
  • Chasing a platform

Influence from Any Seat Is

  • Trusted leadership
  • Honest feedback
  • Courageous conversations
  • Stewardship of vision
  • Legacy over platform
01

Lead Yourself

The seat you're in isn't a waiting room. Influence starts with how you carry the seat you already have.

02

Lead Up

Aligning with the leader above you without losing your own voice. Agreement means something because disagreement was always allowed.

03

Lead Across

Peer influence, earned the same way trust always is. Serve sideways instead of competing for the same room.

04

Lead Down

Leading the people on your team as individuals, not interchangeable roles. Nobody follows a title for long.

05

Serve the Mission

Caring more about results than explanations. Doing the job in front of you, especially the parts nobody's watching.

06

Find Your Seat

Stop measuring your leadership against the person who holds the final word. Your seat has its own definition of success.
